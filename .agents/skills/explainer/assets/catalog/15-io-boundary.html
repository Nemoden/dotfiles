I/O Boundary

A system is a box with a surface. This view draws the surface, then lists every value that crosses it and in which direction. Values are not peers: one born outside and consumed here is untrusted input; one born inside that leaves is a commitment to another party. This answers "what enters, what leaves, and what is trusted?" — the question you ask before you can reason about safety at all.
