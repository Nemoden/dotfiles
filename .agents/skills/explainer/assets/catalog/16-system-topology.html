System Topology

The whole flow as a node-link diagram over swimlanes: each domain (the outside world, our backend, a vendor) is a horizontal band; dataflow runs left-to-right across them. Arrows carry the data — the label on each arrow is what rides it. Long payloads live behind a click, not on the canvas. What we do not own is drawn faded + dashed. Follow one value's chip to light its path through every system; a value that never crosses an auth check lights the whole chain unbroken.
